# LinkedIn playbook

LinkedIn is where clients, founders and other engineers judge {{user_name}}'s work. Posts should read like a practitioner sharing something real, not a marketer.

## Structure
- **Hook (first 1–2 lines, under 200 characters).** Only this shows before "…see more". Lead with the result, the problem or a surprising lesson. No "I'm excited to announce".
- **Body.** The story in short paragraphs of 1–3 lines with a blank line between them: the problem, what was built, what happened, what was learned.
- **Specifics.** One or two real details from the profile: a number, a tool, a choice that was made and why.
- **Close.** One line: a takeaway or a genuine question for readers. Not "Thoughts?" on its own.
- Lists are fine for steps or lessons (3–5 items). Not for everything.
- Links go in the first comment, not the post (they cut reach). Say "link in the comments" if needed.

## Tone
- First person, simple English, friendly and practical. Confident about what was built; humble about what's still being learned.
- Explain technical ideas so a founder could follow them: one plain sentence per concept.
- At most 1–2 emojis, only where they help. None in the hook.
- Never sound like an ad. Show the work; the work sells.

## Hashtags
- 3–5 at the very end, on their own line.
- Mix one broad tag (#AI, #AgenticAI) with specific ones (#MCP, #RAG, #OpenAI).
- Prefer tags from the profile's hashtag list.

## Red lines
- Only facts from the profile and the user's own words. No invented clients, numbers, results or years of experience.
- Never name a private client or share their details without permission.
- No engagement bait ("Comment YES", "Agree?"), no fake urgency, no tagging people for reach.
- No negative posts about past employers, clients or other developers.
- No prices unless a rate is set in the profile.

## Good post ideas
- A project write-up: the problem, the agent design, one thing that went wrong and the fix.
- A lesson from building with agents, MCP or RAG, explained simply.
- A short tutorial: "How I connect X to an AI agent in 3 steps".
- A milestone (certificate, new role, MS progress) with what it taught, not just the news.
