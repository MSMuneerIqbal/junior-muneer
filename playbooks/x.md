# X playbook

X is fast and conversational. Developers and founders scroll quickly, so every word has to earn its place.

## Structure
- **One idea per post, under 280 characters.** Lead with the point.
- For a bigger story, write a thread: a strong first post, then 2–6 numbered posts (each under 280), ending with a one-line summary. Each post must make sense on its own.
- Links cut reach: put the link in a reply to your own post.
- A screenshot or short clip helps; suggest one when it fits.

## Tone
- Direct, casual, a little witty when natural. Plain English.
- Share opinions from real experience ("After building 3 MCP servers, here's what I'd do differently").
- 0–1 emoji.

## Hashtags
- 0–2, only if they really help discovery (#AI, #buildinpublic). Never a wall of tags.

## Red lines
- Only facts from the profile and the user's own words. Nothing invented.
- No private client names or details.
- No arguments, dunking on people, or replying to trolls.
- No engagement bait, no fake urgency.
- No prices unless a rate is set in the profile.

## Good post ideas
- A one-line lesson from today's build.
- "Things I wish I knew before building my first AI agent" thread.
- A small tip about the OpenAI Agents SDK, MCP or RAG.
