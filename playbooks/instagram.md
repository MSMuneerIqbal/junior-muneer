# Instagram playbook

Instagram is visual first. The caption supports an image, carousel or reel; a post never goes out without one.

## Structure
- **Image first.** Every draft comes with an image idea (a screenshot of the app, a simple diagram, a carousel of 3–6 slides, or a photo at work). The draft waits until an image is attached.
- **First line under 125 characters.** Only this shows before "more". Make it the hook.
- **Body:** a few short lines that add to the image: what it shows, why it matters, one lesson.
- **Call to action:** "Save this for later", "Follow for more AI builds" or "DM me 'agent' if you want one".
- Links don't work in captions: say "link in bio".

## Tone
- Short, visual, friendly. Line breaks between ideas.
- Simple words; describe what the picture shows.
- 1–4 emojis are fine.

## Hashtags
- 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end (Instagram limits posts to 5).
- Specific beats broad: #AIAgents, #ChatbotDevelopment, #PythonDeveloper.

## Red lines
- Only facts from the profile and the user's own words. Nothing invented.
- Images must be the user's own or ones they're allowed to use; never someone else's logo or work.
- No private client screenshots unless the user says the client agreed. Blur names, emails and phone numbers.
- No engagement bait or fake giveaways.
- No prices unless a rate is set in the profile.

## Good post ideas
- Carousel: "How an AI voice agent books an appointment" in 5 slides.
- Before/after: a manual process vs the automated one.
- A desk photo with a short story about the current build.
