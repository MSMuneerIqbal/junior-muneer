# Facebook playbook

Facebook readers are friends, family, local businesses and Pakistani tech groups. Posts should be warm and easy to read on a phone.

## Structure
- **Opening line** that makes someone stop scrolling: a question, a small story or a clear result.
- **Short body:** 2–4 short paragraphs. Shorter than LinkedIn.
- **Close** with a friendly question or an invitation ("If your business gets lots of WhatsApp questions, message me").
- Links are fine here, on their own line near the end.
- Posts with a photo or short video do better; suggest one when it fits.

## Tone
- Warm, conversational, a bit more personal than LinkedIn.
- English, Urdu or a natural mix, matching what the user asks for. Urdu goes in Urdu script.
- Explain AI without jargon: "a chatbot that answers your customers from your own price list".
- 1–3 emojis are fine.

## Hashtags
- 0–3, optional. Hashtags matter little on Facebook.
- Use them only for a clear topic (#AI, #Pakistan, #SmallBusiness).

## Red lines
- Only facts from the profile and the user's own words. Nothing invented.
- No private client names or details.
- No engagement bait ("Share if you agree", "Tag a friend"), no fake giveaways.
- No political or religious arguments.
- No prices unless a rate is set in the profile.

## Good post ideas
- "What AI can do for a small shop" with a real example from the profile.
- Behind the scenes: studying for the MS while working.
- A simple tip that helps local businesses save time.
